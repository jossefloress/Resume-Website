PlanDrop Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 31, 2026

PlanDrop ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

Data We Collect

  • Apple ID identifier — Used for authentication via Sign in with Apple. We receive an opaque user ID; we do not access your email unless you choose to share it.
  • Display name — The name you provide during onboarding, shown to friends in the app.
  • Visit status — Your availability mode (open, closed, timed, scheduled), location labels, and optional coordinates stored in Apple's CloudKit.
  • Meetup proposals — Time slots, locations, and votes exchanged via iMessage (stored in the Messages framework, not on our servers).
  • Location data — Used only when you grant permission, to suggest nearby places and show your approximate position to friends on the map. Coordinates are randomized to neighborhood precision (~500m) by default.
  • Notification preferences — Stored locally on your device.

How We Use Your Data

  • To show your visit status to friends you've connected with
  • To suggest meeting times and places based on mutual availability
  • To send local notifications about proposals, nudges, and friend activity
  • To maintain meetup streaks and social memory features

Data Storage

All synced data is stored in Apple's CloudKit infrastructure under your iCloud account. We do not operate our own servers. Local data (preferences, cached statuses) is stored on-device using SwiftData.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties. Visit status data is visible only to users within your configured audience setting (everyone, contacts only, or favorites only).

Your Rights

  • Access & deletion — You can delete your account and all associated data from the Profile settings screen.
  • Location control — You can revoke location permission at any time in iOS Settings. Your map precision can be set to "off" to hide from the map entirely.
  • Notification control — All notification types can be individually toggled in the app's Notification Preferences screen.

Children's Privacy

PlanDrop is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above.
